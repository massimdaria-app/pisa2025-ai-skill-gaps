# First descriptive finding (23 Oct 2026, weighted)

Weighted mean frequency of AI use for schoolwork by within-country ESCS
quintile. Six countries (ARE, AUS, BRA, ESP, GBR, KAZ), PISA 2025,
final student weights W_FSTUWT.

Pooled gradient Q1 -> Q5:
- Draft my writing: 2.43 -> 2.55  (+0.12)
- Help me learn:    2.90 -> 3.38  (+0.49)

The learning-use gradient is four times the substitutive one.

By country, drafting:
- Flat in GBR (2.07 -> 2.08), KAZ (2.97 -> 2.98), ARE (3.23 -> 3.22)
- Rises in AUS (2.24 -> 2.71) and ESP (2.58 -> 2.82)
- BRA rises to Q4 then falls at Q5

By country, learning: rises in all six, from +0.16 (GBR) to +0.84 (AUS).

Reading: the socio-economic gap is not in who outsources the work.
In half the countries substitutive use does not vary with ESCS at all,
while learning use rises everywhere.

Also notable: GBR has the lowest levels of both uses in the sample
(2.07 and 2.51 vs 3.23 and 3.59 in ARE).

Unweighted means run ~0.2 higher and understate the gap (0.14 vs 0.41).
Weights are used from here on.

NOT a finding: drafting dips at Q5 in BRA, KAZ, ARE but not in the other
three — 0.03-0.09 on an ordinal scale, almost certainly noise. Revisit
once standard errors exist.

CAVEATS: ordinal scale averaged as continuous; no standard errors (BRR
pending); no controls; descriptive, no causal claim.
