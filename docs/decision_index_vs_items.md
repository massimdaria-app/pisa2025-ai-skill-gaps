# Decision — index or separate items (23 Oct 2026)

Pairwise correlations among the four ST438 items (pooled, six countries):

|                      | Draft | Summarise | Research | Learn |
|----------------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Draft my writing     | 1.00  | 0.69      | 0.68     | 0.60  |
| Summarise a text     | 0.69  | 1.00      | 0.70     | 0.61  |
| Preliminary research | 0.68  | 0.70      | 1.00     | 0.69  |
| Help me learn        | 0.60  | 0.61      | 0.69     | 1.00  |

All four correlate moderately (0.60-0.70): a common "uses AI at all"
factor exists, but is not strong enough to treat the items as one
measure.

Decision: keep the items separate in the main specification.
The weakest pair in the matrix is drafting x learning (0.60) — exactly
the two poles of the research question. Items that are conceptually
adjacent (summarising x research, research x learning) correlate more
highly. This is evidence that the substitutive and the learning mode
capture distinct behaviour rather than one latent frequency.

A simple mean index of the four items is kept as a robustness check:
if the ESCS gradient survives on the index but the divergence between
items disappears, that would argue against the interpretation.

Note: correlations computed on complete pairs, unweighted. Weighted
correlations pending.
