# Dependent variable — resolved (23 Oct 2026)

Main outcome: MATHEMATICS (PV1-10 MATH). Reading as robustness.

LDW was considered and rejected as the main outcome after reading the
PISA 2025 Learning in the Digital World Assessment Framework
(Second Draft, OECD, October 2023).

Three reasons, all from the framework itself:

1. The proficiency scale covers only computational problem-solving
   practices: conduct experiments, analyse data, build and debug
   computational artefacts. The subscales in the data (CMOD Modelling,
   CPRO Programming) reflect these. This is computational thinking,
   not learning capacity.

2. Self-regulated learning processes (monitor progress, evaluate
   performance, maintain motivation) are reported as separate
   indicators and do NOT enter the achievement scale — precisely the
   part of the construct that motivated considering LDW.

3. FAQ, p.4: "Although an important aspect of learning in the digital
   world, the PISA 2025 test will not measure students' ability to
   search for and evaluate online information." ST438Q02 (preliminary
   research) and ST436Q16 (assessing chatbot output quality) are about
   exactly that capability, so relating them to the LDW scale would
   claim a link to something the test deliberately does not capture.

LDW is retained as a third, exploratory outcome, labelled honestly as
computational problem solving, reported in a separate paragraph.
